Effect of Si, Mn, V and B on the Electrical Resistivity of 8030 Aluminum Rods#

The paper finds that Si, Mn, and V all increase the electrical resistivity of 8030 aluminum rods, while B has a much smaller effect. Although Si is present at a higher concentration and varies over a larger composition range, its resistivity contribution is only slightly greater than those of Mn and V.

Main conclusions

  • Silicon (Si): Produces the largest increase among the principal alloying elements, but only by a small margin over Mn and V.

  • Manganese (Mn) and vanadium (V): Also substantially increase resistivity, despite their lower concentrations.

  • Boron (B): Has a comparatively weak effect on resistivity.

  • The results indicate that resistivity cannot be estimated from alloy concentration alone; the specific scattering strength of each solute in aluminum is important.

The physical explanation is electron scattering: dissolved alloying atoms disturb the aluminum crystal lattice and reduce the mean free path of conduction electrons. The effect is influenced by atomic size mismatch, electronic structure, and whether the element remains in solid solution or forms intermetallic/boride phases.

The article is “Effect of Si, Mn, V and B on the Electrical Resistivity of 8030 Aluminum Rods”.
